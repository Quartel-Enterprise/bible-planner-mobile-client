Read the whole Bible in one year — with the plan, the Bible text and the study in one app.

Bible Planner turns your Bible reading into a daily 1-year plan, and brings the whole Bible with it. Open the app, read the day's passage, tick off what you read, and watch a year of reading fill up week by week.

📖 THE WHOLE BIBLE, OFFLINE
Download the translation you read — King James Version, New International Version, English Standard Version or the World English Bible — and read with no connection at all. All 66 books, chapter by chapter, inside the reading plan itself.

✅ TWO 1-YEAR READING PLANS
• Books order — Genesis to Revelation, in the traditional order
• Chronological order — the events of the Bible as they happened
Each plan is split into 52 weeks of short daily readings.

🔥 PROGRESS AND STREAK
Track your Bible reading by book, by chapter and by verse. The plan card shows your streak, the week you are on, and how much of the year is left.

✍️ HIGHLIGHTS AND NOTES, ON THE VERSE
Highlight verses in colour and write your notes straight onto the passage. Everything is saved to your account and synced across your phone, your tablet and any device you sign in on.

🤖 A STUDY OF EVERY READING
Each day's reading comes with a study: an overview of the passage, its historical context, and questions to sit with. Plus a chat that already knows the day's text, so you can ask whatever the passage raises.

📚 ALL 66 BOOKS, TRACKED
Per-chapter progress on every book, favourites and search — read the Bible outside the plan whenever you want.

🎨 YOURS
Light and dark themes, Material You dynamic colour, adjustable font and text size, in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

GETTING STARTED
1. Pick your reading plan — books order or chronological
2. Download your Bible translation
3. Read the day's passage and mark it read
4. Come back tomorrow and keep the streak

FREE AND PRO
The reading plan, the full Bible, progress tracking and highlights are free. Pro unlocks unlimited AI day study and chat, unlimited notes, and the full highlight palette.
